Offline games

Applies to Bolt Away, Brick Slide, Cybernetic Rolls, Isletopia, It Doesn't End, Knights Grandiose, Mad Spins and Space Donkey.

  • These games collect, transmit and share no personal information.
  • They save your settings and progress only in the app's private storage on your device. That data never leaves the device and is removed when you uninstall the game.
  • No account, sign-in, ads, analytics, crash reporting or in-app purchases.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.

Knight's Glory

Knight's Glory works like the offline games above: it collects nothing, has no account, ads or analytics, and saves progress only on your device. The one difference is that when it starts, it downloads its open-source 3D graphics library (three.js) from the public jsDelivr content network. As with any web download, jsDelivr receives standard technical request details such as your IP address and device type. We do not receive or store that information.

HomeVault

HomeVault is a home inventory, receipt and warranty tracker. It needs an account, and your vault is stored on our server so it can be shared with your household.

What it collects

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. If you choose Google Sign-In, we receive your name, email address and Google account identifier from Google.
  • Household: the members you invite, and their names and email addresses.
  • Your vault: items, locations, notes, photos you take or choose, receipt images and the details read from them (store, date, items, amounts), warranties, and item history.
  • Scans: the QR labels and product barcodes you scan with the camera.
  • Questions you type into the "Ask" feature.

How it's used and shared

  • Stored on our server at homevault.prundaru.ca and shown only to you and the household members you invite.
  • Receipt images and "Ask" questions may be sent to Anthropic (the Claude API) to read receipt details and answer questions. Anthropic processes the data to provide that service and does not use it to train its models.
  • Camera is used only for photos and scanning. Bluetooth is used only to connect to a label printer you choose. On some Android versions, Android requires the location permission to search for Bluetooth printers. HomeVault does not read or store your location.

TripVault: Trips & Points

TripVault organizes trips and tracks loyalty points. It works without an account, keeping everything on your device. Data leaves your device only when you use one of the features below.

  • Sign-in and sync (optional): if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and account identifier. Your trips, bookings, loyalty balances and settings are then synced to Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) so they are available on your other devices.
  • Loyalty program logins you save are kept on your device in Android's encrypted storage. They leave the device only if you use automatic balance refresh (see below).
  • Email inbox (optional): if you turn it on, the app gives you a private forwarding address. Booking confirmations and statements you forward are received through Cloudflare Email Routing and held on our server (inbox.prundaru.ca) so the app can import them.
  • Fare comparison and automatic balance refresh (optional): these work only after you connect an automation server in Settings. When you search, the routes, dates and cabin you choose are sent to that server to look up fares. When you refresh balances, the username and password for each selected loyalty program are sent over an encrypted connection to that server, which uses them only to sign in and read your balance for that one job, and does not save them.
  • Notifications are scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

Vital Sync

Vital Sync brings your health and fitness data into one dashboard and records workouts. It needs an account.

What it collects

  • Account details: email address and name, and an account identifier from Google or Apple if you sign in with them.
  • Health and fitness data that you choose to connect or record, such as steps, heart rate, weight, sleep, energy, distance, blood oxygen and workouts. It comes from Health Connect on your phone, from services you link (Strava, Fitbit, Withings), or from the app's own workout recorder.
  • Precise location while you record a workout, including in the background while a recording is running (shown by a persistent notification). It is used to draw your route and measure distance. Location is not collected when you are not recording.
  • Physical activity sensor data during recording, to detect movement.
  • Voice recordings you choose to make, and their transcripts.
  • Lab results, if you choose to connect a BC Health Gateway account.
  • Social content: activities you choose to share, and comments and kudos you post.

How it's used and shared

  • Stored in your private account space in Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions) and used only to provide the app's features.
  • Activities are private unless you share them to the feed. Shared activities, comments and kudos are visible to other Vital Sync users.
  • Linked services (Strava, Fitbit, Withings, BC Health Gateway) exchange data with us only after you authorize them. Their access tokens are kept on our backend and never shown to other users. Disconnecting a service stops syncing.
  • AI and transcription features are optional and use an API key that you supply. When you use them, the data needed for that request (for example a voice recording or a health summary) is sent directly to the provider you configured: OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, OpenRouter or AssemblyAI. That provider handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • Health data is never used for advertising, sold, or used to decide eligibility for credit, insurance or employment.
  • Vital Sync's use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

DealHarbour

DealHarbour is the mobile app for our private real estate dashboard. It is intended only for authorized users with access to that dashboard.

  • The app displays the dashboard hosted at realty.prundaru.ca, which is protected by Cloudflare Access. Information you view or enter there is stored on our server.
  • Camera and files are used only when you choose to take a photo or attach a document to upload to the dashboard.
  • The app itself contains no ads or analytics.

Service providers

We share information only with the providers named above, and only so they can run the features that need them:

  • Google Firebase for sign-in and cloud storage (TripVault, Vital Sync)
  • Cloudflare for secure access and email routing to our servers
  • The automation server you connect in TripVault, for fare searches and balance refresh
  • Anthropic for reading receipts and answering questions (HomeVault)
  • AI and transcription providers that you configure (Vital Sync)

Our servers and these providers may process data in Canada, the United States or other countries. We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Security and retention

All data sent between our apps and our servers or providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access tokens and saved logins are kept in encrypted storage. We keep account data for as long as your account exists, and delete it when you delete your account or ask us to.

Deleting your account and data

  • Offline games and Knight's Glory: uninstall the app. Nothing is held anywhere else.
  • HomeVault, TripVault, Vital Sync and DealHarbour: use the in-app delete or export option where available, or email g@ghms.me from the address on your account, naming the app. We will delete your account and its data within 30 days and confirm when it's done. Copies in backups are removed within a further 30 days.
  • For TripVault without sign-in, all data is on your device: uninstalling or clearing the app's data removes it.

You can also ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct it, at the same address.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If our apps start handling information differently, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above before the change takes effect.
